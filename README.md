# Fitness Subscription Analytics

## Customer Cohort Analysis
Trend: Customer cancellations and churn happen gradually over time, but the steepest drop-off occurs within the first 1 to 3 months of subscription.

Insight: Once customers cross the 3-month mark, retention rates stabilize into a reliable, long-term retention curve, with average retention hovering around 50%–60% at Month 3 across cohorts.

## Revenue Forecast
The 12-month time-series forecast represents total transaction revenue generated across the platform, rather than recurring subscription revenue exclusively.

Projections: Transaction revenue maintains a strong upward trajectory through 2026, scaling steadily from approximately $230K past $300K. The forecast exhibits consistent linear growth without seasonal ups and downs.

## CAC vs. LTV Analysis
2024 Cohort: Recovers its customer acquisition costs by Month 3, establishing a solid foundation for long-term customer profitability.

2025 Cohort: Recovers its customer acquisition costs by Month 5. While it takes slightly longer to reach break-even compared to the 2024 group, the 2025 cohort benefits from a higher overall lifetime value (LTV) curve.
Strategic Benefits of the 2025 LTV Curve: A higher overall LTV trajectory delivers greater per-customer profitability over the long term, provides broader financial headroom to absorb acquisition expenses, and signals stronger customer engagement and monetization potential for stakeholders.
