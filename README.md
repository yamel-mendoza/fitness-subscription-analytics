# Fitness Subscription Analytics

## Customer Cohort Analysis
Trend: Customer cancellations and churn happen gradually over time, but the most significant drop-off occurs within the first 1 to 3 months of subscription.
Insight: Once customers make it past the 3-month mark, retention rates stabilize significantly into a steady, long-term retention curve.

## Revenue Forecast
Projections: Subscription revenue is projected to maintain a strong, upward trajectory over the next 12 months, scaling from roughly $230K up past $300K.
Seasonality: The forecast exhibits steady, linear growth without sharp cyclical fluctuations, indicating consistent business expansion rather than seasonal volatility.

## CAC vs. LTV Analysis
Break-Even Point: Both the 2024 and 2025 customer cohorts recover their customer acquisition costs extremely efficiently—reaching the break-even threshold right around Month 1 as cumulative lifetime value surpasses acquisition spending.
